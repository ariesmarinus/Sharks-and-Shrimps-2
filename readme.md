# Sharks and Shrimps
This is a multiplayer game where each player controls a team of animals consisting of a Shark, a Fish and a Shrimp, and the team that has more animals escaped the field wins.

### **Full Rules** 
Each player moves an animal of their team one square at a time in the order `Shark -> Fish -> Shrimp`, taking turns with the opponent between each animal. Each animal must defeat their target animal in the opponent team. Shark eats Fish, Fish eats Shrimp and Shrimp tickles Shark. Animals cannot move to the square where they or another animal from the same team have already been. If an animal has no squares they can move to they become trapped and exit the game. An animal can escape the field if they already defeated their target animal in the opposite team or the target animal became trapped or escaped the field. Once all available animals escape, the team who has more animals left wins.
